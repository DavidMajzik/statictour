The problem

Every virtual tour product is a hosted subscription: Kuula, Matterport, Theasys, Lapentor, 3DVista. You pay monthly, the tour lives on their servers, and if you stop paying – or they pivot – it's gone. A dentist, a hotel, a church or a gallery needs one tour that keeps working for five years. That's not a subscription problem. It's a static-files problem.

How it works

  1. Drop in 360° panoramas – equirectangular JPG/PNG from any 360° camera (Theta, Insta360, GoPro Max…). Resized and re-encoded in the browser.
  2. Place arrows on doors – click a door, pick the room it leads to. Set the entry view per room. The editor warns you about rooms you can't reach.
  3. Download tour.zip – unzip, upload the tour/ folder with FTP, cPanel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, S3, whatever. Open /tour/. Done.

Embedding is one iframe:

<iframe src="/tour/" allow="fullscreen; gyroscope; accelerometer" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Keep the ZIP – re-open it in the editor any time to add rooms or move arrows.
